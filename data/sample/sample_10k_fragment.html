ANNUAL REPORT ON FORM 10-K

SampleCorp Inc. | Fiscal Year Ended December 31, 2024

ITEM 1. BUSINESS

SampleCorp Inc. is a technology company that develops and sells consumer electronics, software, and online services. The company was founded in 2001 and is headquartered in San Jose, California. The company's products include smartphones, tablets, personal computers, wearables, and accessories.

The company operates through four geographic segments: Americas, Europe, Greater China, Japan, and Rest of Asia Pacific. Net sales for fiscal year 2024 were approximately $400 billion, representing a 3% increase compared to the prior year.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The following risk factors could materially affect our business, financial condition, or results of operations. These risks are not the only risks we face.

Supply Chain Risks: Our products depend on components and materials from a limited number of suppliers. Disruptions to our supply chain — including from geopolitical tensions, natural disasters, or pandemics — could significantly reduce our ability to manufacture and deliver products. We rely on single-source suppliers for several critical components including custom processors and display panels.

Competition: We face intense competition from companies that have significant resources, global reach, and established brand recognition. Competitors may develop products or services that are superior to ours or that achieve greater market acceptance.

Regulatory Risks: We are subject to complex and evolving laws and regulations in the United States and internationally, including antitrust, privacy, data security, and environmental regulations. Non-compliance could result in significant fines, penalties, or reputational harm.

ITEM 2. PROPERTIES

Our principal executive offices are located at 1 Infinite Loop, Cupertino, California. We lease and own numerous facilities across the globe for research and development, manufacturing support, retail, and corporate functions.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS

The following discussion should be read in conjunction with our consolidated financial statements and related notes. This section contains forward-looking statements that involve risks and uncertainties.

Revenue: Net sales increased $12.3 billion, or 3%, to $400.2 billion during fiscal 2024 compared to $387.9 billion in fiscal 2023. The increase was driven primarily by higher iPhone and Services revenue, partially offset by lower Mac and iPad sales.

Gross Margin: Gross margin increased to 46.2% in fiscal 2024 compared to 44.1% in fiscal 2023. The improvement reflects favorable commodity prices and a higher mix of Services revenue, which carries higher gross margins than our Products segment.

Operating Expenses: Research and development expense increased 8% to $29.9 billion in fiscal 2024, reflecting our continued investment in innovation across our product and services portfolio.
